Good to know & troubleshooting

This is a temporary, receive-only inbox. Some websites block disposable email addresses; receiving their OTP is not guaranteed. SMS codes cannot arrive here. Avoid using temporary addresses for important accounts you need to recover later.

Your current inbox credentials are saved only in this browser on this website. Mail.tm or DropMail processes and stores incoming emails, depending on the selected domain. DropMail inboxes can expire after 10 minutes without activity; old emails are lost. Keep this tab visible while waiting. DropMail tokens also expire and may need renewal. Clearing browser data, private browsing, changing the website URL, or changing devices can lose access. This is not permanent email storage.

No email yet? Check the full address, wait a little, then press Refresh. To test delivery, send a normal email from your own Gmail or other email account to this address. If that arrives but a website’s OTP does not, that website may be blocking disposable emails.

Connection error? Check your internet and try again. Your email provider may be unavailable or rate-limiting requests. The page waits before retrying. A name-already-used message means you need a different name. Address changes create a new inbox and retain its details on this browser. Saving an inbox here does not prevent the provider from expiring it. DropMail’s Forget inbox removes only the saved details on this browser; provider emails remain until the session expires.

Emails are displayed as text. Remote images, scripts and attachments are not loaded. Open verification links only when you trust the sender. Code suggestions are guesses: check the original message. This website is hosted on GitHub Pages and connects to the selected email provider through your configured Cloudflare Worker. Inbox credentials and messages pass through that server without being saved there. Outgoing email is not supported.
